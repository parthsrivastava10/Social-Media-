## 1. Objective & north star

- **North star (90 days):** **raw follower count, 0 → 10k.** Chosen for **investor optics** ahead
  of a funding push.
- **Real business lever underneath it:** **first-note activation** (only ~1 paid user, ~150 total,
  mostly team; low activation). Followers are the optics metric; activation is where the business is
  won. **Pillar 4 (Product/Activation) is the bridge** between vanity reach and real value — never drop it.
- **Honest guardrail:** 0→10k in 90 days realistically requires **1–3 genuine breakout reels
  (100k+ views)**. Optimize for *swings at a breakout*, not 90 perfectly-average posts. The strategy
  conservatively assumes **near-zero proven external traction** (treat US demand as a bet, not a fact).

## 2. The audit & the core diagnosis

Data pulled via the Windsor.ai **`instagram_public`** connector (account `onekey_notes`). At audit
time: **38 followers · following 2 · 17 posts.** Avg **34.3 likes** / **3.5 comments** per post —
avg likes ≈ **90% of total follower count**, i.e. posts travel far beyond followers.

> **CORE DIAGNOSIS (the spine of everything):**
> **Reach works. Follow-conversion is broken.** Content reaches well beyond followers, but almost
> none convert to follows. The fix is **NOT distribution** — it's the **profile + follow-CTAs +
> consistency.**

**Supporting findings:**
- **Reels (avg 38.4 likes) >> Carousels (avg 15.0).** The 3 carousels were the 3 worst posts and the
  most overtly product-pitchy. **Reels = founder-POV/relatable = strongest. Carousels/announcements = weakest.**
- Best posts: "designer or developer, OneKey is for both," "I asked 30 founders how they capture ideas,"
  "rebuild brick by brick" — founder POV + research + relatability.
- **Caption typos** ("we build," "track the track task") — brand-damaging for a product whose promise is
  "speak messy → get polished output." Captions must be flawless.
- **Following only 2 accounts** → the founder community (free targeted reach) is going unused.
- **Cadence was broken** — a burst, then an ~11-day blackout, then sporadic. Consistency is itself a lever.

**Metrics that matter (in priority order):** reach / new accounts reached → saves & shares (quality
signal) → follower growth (north star). ⚠️ The public connector **cannot** see reach, saves,
impressions, profile visits, or link taps — **Parth must feed Instagram Insights weekly** for the
Friday audit to pick real winners.

## 3. Audience / ICP

**Primary ICP:** **early-stage founders whose minds run all day and who keep losing thoughts before
they can capture them.** Broader: high-output people (founders, execs, creators, consultants) whose
thinking is their main asset. **Litmus test:** *"Do you end your day knowing you lost at least one
thought that mattered?"* → yes = the customer.

**Signals:** sends WhatsApp voice notes to themselves; notes app with 200+ never-opened notes; best
ideas arrive in the shower / driving / at 2am. **AI adoption is a signal, not the identity** — do not
frame the customer as "AI users."

**Geography (target by clock, don't split content):** US-coded voice/positioning by default; India for
volume/recognition. Single posting window **~8–11pm IST** (= US morning + India late-night).

## 4. Positioning & category discipline

- Anchor in the **second brain / capture → structure → act** narrative; OneKey is "the layer between
  your raw thought and a usable output."
- **Category discipline (carried from ASO):** stay in the **"second brain / AI notes / capture ideas"**
  cluster. **Avoid "transcribe / dictation / recorder" framing** — it attracts wrong-fit users and kills
  conversion.

## 5. The 4 content pillars + weekly mix

Default weekly mix: **2 / 2 / 2 / 1.**

1. **"30 Founders" (FLAGSHIP, recurring)** — the one proven format. Structure: *"I asked 30 founders
   [one question]. Nobody saw the answer coming."* → 2–3 surprising answers → soft tie to OneKey →
   follow CTA. Curiosity + social proof + authority. **Must be based on REAL founder conversations —
   never fabricate.**
2. **Voice of OneKey / Founder POV** — direct-to-camera lessons, contrarian takes, build moments
   (Hormozi/Paritosh register). Uses "we" for the product.
3. **Pain-point relatability** — the ICP's daily reality (2am brain won't shut up, lost thoughts,
   post-meeting amnesia). Highest-share content.
4. **Product-in-action / Activation bridge** — the ~10-second speak→clean-note transformation. Doubles
   as the activation fix by pre-teaching the first-note action.

## 6. What's working / not working

- **Working:** founder-POV reels; the "30 founders" research hook; relatable pain; research-backed content.
- **Not working:** carousels; hard-sell/announcement posts; inconsistent cadence; caption typos; generic
  "link in bio" CTAs.

## 7. Follow-conversion mechanics (the actual growth levers)

- **End every reel with a follow REASON**, not a generic "link in bio." (Give them a concrete reason the
  next reel is worth following for.)
- **Swap "drop hi" → "comment [word] for the link"** — drives comments + installs + DMs.
- **First 60 minutes:** reply to every comment within the first hour (boosts reach toward Explore).
- **Pre-post warmup:** 10 min commenting on 5–10 founder / AI-tools accounts before posting.
- **Activation bridge:** product reels show the exact first-note flow, so installs actually activate.

## 8. Profile rebuild (target state)

- **Photo:** Parth's face (face beats logo for follow-conversion).
- **Name field (bold/searchable):** pair Parth + OneKey + a keyword (e.g. "Parth · AI Notes for
  Founders"). Keep OneKey primary; don't imply sole ownership of building.
- **Handle:** keep **@onekey_notes** (already indexed).
- **Bio (3 lines — hook / what / CTA):** second-brain/messy-thoughts hook → what it does (speak →
  structured note) → explicit download CTA + the live universal link (redirects to App/Play store by device).
- **Highlights:** `▶ DEMO` · `BUILD` · `30 FOUNDERS` · `FREE`.
- **Pinned (3):** best "30 Founders" reel · a crisp product demo · a founder-POV manifesto reel.

## 9. Operating rules

- **Batch-film weekly** (one Saturday session, ~8–10 pieces, ~2 outfit changes); the editor works a queue
  a week ahead. **Never film daily** — that caused the prior blackout.
- **Post natively in-app**, ~8–11pm IST.
- **Captions = product proof:** zero typos; run every caption through OneKey before posting.

## 10. Paid deployment ($100–200 budget)

Only **boost proven organic winners** (reels that already travel). Objective = **profile visits / follows**
(not link clicks). ~$15–25 increments; target US + India, ages ~24–40, startup/productivity interests.
**Never spend on day one; never use paid to find product-market fit.**

## 11. 90-day phasing

- **Wk 1–2 — Foundation:** rebuild profile, lock daily cadence, find voice.
- **Wk 3–6 — Volume & signal:** identify which formats travel; first small paid tests.
- **Wk 7–10 — Scale winners:** weight the calendar toward top formats; deploy most paid.
- **Wk 11–13 — Compound & push:** re-cut best performers; final paid sprint.

## 13. Worked example — integrating a product update (reference for the calendar logic)

When the redesign landed, three updates "earned a reel": **cutting features → a founder-POV story**
(highest follow-conversion); **the new recording animation → best visual asset** (and activation bridge);
**the full before/after reveal → highest-reach format** (absorbs minor updates as supporting beats).
Minor items (font, tags, note-cards) appeared only inside the reveal; **Today's Focus** was held for a
dedicated "capture → act" reel the following week. The three update reels were slotted into the soonest
unfilmed week; the displaced pain reel cascaded to the next week, where the mix rebalanced back toward
2/2/2/1. (The mechanical rules live in `skills/calendar-logic/SKILL.md`.)
