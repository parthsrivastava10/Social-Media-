# context/voice.md — How OneKey Sounds

> The voice spec for every script, caption, and hook. The **tone + the Voice-of-OneKey rule** are
> canonical (from the audit). The **feel, hooks, and captions banks** are reference texture lifted
> from earlier script work — use them for flavor and pattern, not as a rigid template. The
> script-writer reads this file plus `brand-strategy.md` before writing.

---

## 1. Tone

- **Clean & minimal. Expert & authoritative. Founder conviction.**
- **FaceTime / founder-to-founder energy** — talking to one specific overwhelmed founder, not
  performing for an audience or lecturing as an expert.
- High-status, never cringe. Serious operator who teaches with real value. (Register: Hormozi
  value-density × Paritosh founder energy.)

## 2. THE VOICE-OF-ONEKEY RULE (non-negotiable — applies to every script)

- **The product = "we."** Parth is a co-founder, so "we built / we shipped / we deleted features /
  we made it for ourselves" is honest, full ownership.
- **Market observations / opinions = "I."** His personal read on founders, productivity, building
  ("I asked 30 founders…", "I think writing kills the raw thought").
- **Never claim personal engineering/design authorship** — no "I coded this," "I designed this."
  Building and design are "we" (that's the CTO / Design Lead's craft; Parth owns the company-level "we").

## 3. The feel (structural reference from prior script work)

- **Pain before product, always.** Make the viewer feel the problem before OneKey is named. Never open on the product.
- **Hot take / hook first.** The first line must challenge a belief or make someone feel called out.
- **1–3 points max.** One idea, owned fully. Concise beats complete every time.
- **Planned but off-the-cuff.** An outline exists; the delivery feels spontaneous.
- **Close is a door left open, not a hard CTA.** "We're just getting started" lands harder than any
  product description or "go download this."
- **Raw > over-polished.** Parth's natural, conversational hooks beat tightened rewrites. Rough cut,
  closed captions, jump cuts on emotional beats.

## 4. Do's

- Lead with a strong, **honest** hook (uncomfortably specific > clever).
- Teach with real value — give away the playbook.
- **End on a follow REASON**, not a generic "link in bio." (And swap "drop hi" → "comment [word] for the link.")
- Base every "30 founders" reel on **REAL** conversations — never fabricate data.
- Show the **real product flow** in product reels (the activation bridge).
- **Zero-typo captions** — run every caption through OneKey before posting (captions are product proof).
- Preserve the **proprietary phrases** verbatim (§6).

## 5. Don'ts

- No corporate / stiff / press-release tone.
- No emoji overload (minimal, purposeful only).
- No hype / buzzwords ("revolutionary," "game-changer").
- No clickbait — hooks must be honest, not bait-and-switch.
- No dancing; no second person on camera by default.
- **No "transcribe / dictation / recorder" framing** — wrong category, wrong-fit users.
- Don't open on the product; don't let a script sound like a pitch (the moment it does, it breaks).

## 6. Proprietary language (use verbatim — never paraphrase)

- "capture layer" — the category positioning
- "fuzzy thoughts" — raw, unstructured human thought
- "organised forgetting" / "capturing without resurfacing is just organised forgetting"
- "notes apps are graveyards"
- "the gap between thinking, capturing and resurfacing"
- "one brain, one place" — the sync line
- "you speak. OneKey handles everything else." — the endgame

## 7. Reference hooks bank (patterns, not scripts)

**"I asked 30 founders" (flagship):**
- "I asked 30 founders one question. The answer broke my heart a little."
- "I asked 30 founders how they capture their ideas. Not one had a real answer."
- "30 founders. Same broken system."

**"If you [X], save this":**
- "If you send WhatsApp voice notes to yourself, save this."
- "If your notes app has 200 notes you've never opened, save this."
- "If you've ever forgotten an idea on the way to your desk, save this."

**Hot take:**
- "Your notes app is lying to you. What's in there is not what you actually thought."
- "Writing doesn't capture thoughts. It autopsies them."
- "Completed tasks disappearing is the worst thing productivity apps do to you."

**Mentor / story:**
- "My mentor told me this when I was failing at my first startup — you get a golden window of 2 to 3
  hours a day where your thinking is actually clear."
