## 1. The one-sentence definition

OneKey (stylized **1Key**) is **the infrastructure layer between human thought and AI action** —
capturing every thought the moment it happens, structuring it instantly, and taking
accountability for making sure it resurfaces and gets done.

**Core positioning line:** *"The layer between your raw thought and a usable output — with the
accountability of making sure it resurfaces exactly when you need it."*

It is **not a notes app.** Notes apps are graveyards — places where thoughts go to be forgotten.
OneKey is a system with a memory: it captures, structures, and follows through.

## 2. The problem

Human thought is **non-linear**. Every productivity system ever built assumes the opposite —
that you'll sit down, open an app, and structure your thoughts before you capture them. That
assumption is wrong. The best ideas arrive at 11pm, in the shower, mid-meeting, on a walk — raw,
unstructured, contextless. The whole stack (Notion, Obsidian, Slack, transcription tools) was
built for structured input from a seated human; none of it was built to catch a thought in motion.

The gap between *having* a valuable thought and actually *doing* something with it has never had
real infrastructure. The cost is the thought itself.

**Investor-approved problem framings:**
- "Every day you have thoughts worth millions. And you lose them to a notes app you never open again."
- "The gap between having a valuable thought and actually doing something with it — that gap has
  never had infrastructure. Until now."

## 3. The mechanism: Capture → Structure → Execute

1. **Capture** — Open the app or press a key on Mac. Speak, any language, no formatting. Raw thought in.
2. **Structure** — The **AI Enhancer** reads the *intent behind the words* (not just the words) and
   transforms the dump into what it needs to become: task list, email, LinkedIn post, meeting summary.
   It is **not speech-to-text and not a summary** — the sweet spot between, preserving your language,
   energy, and intent while removing the mess.
3. **Execute** — Speak a defined **trigger word** mid-note; OneKey asks permission and fires an
   automated workflow (webhook). A spoken thought becomes an executed action without touching a screen.

Positioning for *how* it solves it: *"Every AI tool in the world needs structured input. OneKey is
the only product that extracts it from raw human thought — in real time."*

## 4. The ecosystem (products)

  Summary template; unlimited free custom templates; webhook integrations; task-list tracker.
  *Live on App Store + Play Store (MVP).* Use case: on-the-go capture, away from the desk.
- **OneKey Mac App** — system-wide voice-to-text layer. Press a key → speak → text appears exactly
  where the cursor is, across any app (Gmail, Notion, Slack, Claude). *Live.* Use case: mid-work
  capture at the desk.
- **Sync** *(in progress)* — both apps connected so every capture lives in one place. "One brain. One place."
- **Widget & Watch** `[FUTURE/horizon]` — instant capture without opening the app; OneKey on the wrist.
- **Capture Ring** `[FUTURE]` — frictionless capture without reaching for a phone.
- **AI Agent Marketplace** `[FUTURE — vision only, do NOT claim as live]` — a fleet of purpose-built
  agents that take structured thought and execute it. "You speak. OneKey handles everything else."

Ecosystem line: *"No other tool covers the complete surface area of human thought across every
device and every context simultaneously."*

## 5. Core features

1. **Frictionless voice capture** — speak raw, in any language, any mess.
2. **AI Enhancer** — transforms a brain dump into a clean note *without* stripping intent, energy,
   or original language. (Not a summary, not a transcript — articulation that keeps the version
   that mattered.)
3. **Task List Tracker** — auto-pulls actionable items from the dump; **completed tasks stay visible
   with a strikethrough** (intentional *motivation design*, not a bug — seeing crossed tasks = proof
   of movement; backed by the 30-founders research).
4. **Webhook integration** — spoken trigger words fire automated workflows.
5. **Unlimited free custom templates** — users build their own output formats (email, LinkedIn post,
   Slack/WhatsApp reply assistant, etc.). "You define the output."

- **Slack** — communication, not capture.
- **Whispr Flow / Otter.ai / AudioPen / Superwhisper / Mem** — transcription / meeting notes /

*"Every other tool captures your thought and forgets it. OneKey captures it, structures it, and
takes responsibility for it."*

## 11. The vision `[FUTURE — frame as the architecture being built toward, not live]`

**Today:** OneKey captures your thought and structures it. **Tomorrow:** OneKey dispatches it.
A trajectory of **capture layer → execution layer → the operating system for human thought.**

- Phase — **Capture via ring**: every thought captured the moment it arrives, without reaching for a phone.
- Phase — **Execute with agents**: captured thoughts worked on automatically by personal AI agents.
- Phase — **Expand to team**: hire pre-built agents from the marketplace; scale intelligence without headcount.

Vision one-liner: *"Capture every thought. Execute every idea. Automatically."*
Vision statement: *"Today you think. Tomorrow you forget. We're building a world where everything
you think gets done."*
